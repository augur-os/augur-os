# What is the atomic unit of Augur?

The atomic unit is the **brain** — not the agent, not the model, not the
orchestration layer. The brain is the owned, git-versioned context an agent reads;
it is composed per node (instructions, decisions, knowledge, skills) and compounds
work back bottom-up so that agents and models remain swappable while the brain
persists as the durable asset. See [[the-compound-brain-atomic-unit]].
