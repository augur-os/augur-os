# How should Refactor And Quality Enforcement Actions be used?

## Summary

Guidance for action-layer quality enforcement, refactor, and structural cleanup workflows.

## Answer

Use this concept when the work is clearly about enforcing standards or surfacing structural cleanup: lint, formatting, alignment, plugin lint, vault hygiene, refactor, technical debt, or protocol validation. These actions expose the quality surface quickly, while the command families provide the deeper proof and administrative control once the direction is clear.
