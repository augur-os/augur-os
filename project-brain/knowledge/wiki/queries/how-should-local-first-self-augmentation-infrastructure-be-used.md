# How should Local-First Self-Augmentation Infrastructure be used?

## Summary

A reusable answer for applying [[concepts/local-first-self-augmentation-infrastructure]].

## Answer

Local-first self-augmentation joins owned user context with bounded AI development workflows, readable files, MCP tools, and portable model clients.

Use [[concepts/local-first-self-augmentation-infrastructure]] as the source-backed synthesis page before returning to raw evidence.

## Evidence

- `document:venture-augur/IntelSubmit/augur-intel-edge-ai-collateral.md`: Augur is local-first personal AI infrastructure that moves cognitive AI workloads — knowledge management, task automation, memory, and decision support — from cloud services to the user's own device. Intel collateral states the cloud-to-device workload migration claim.
- `document:venture-augur/IntelSubmit/augur-intel-form-answers.md`: Their data never leaves their machine. They can switch AI providers (Claude, GPT, Llama, Qwen) without losing anything Form answer connects sovereignty with model portability.
- `document:venture-augur/IntelSubmit/augur-intel-submission-1.pptx`: MCP-first: LLM does thin orchestration, logic lives in tools. Low token cost = local models work. Deck source identifies MCP as the execution architecture behind local operation.
- `document:venture-augur/IntelSubmit/augur.pptx`: Your second brain runs on your PC. Any AI model. Full ownership. Pitch deck condenses the ownership and model-interchangeability message.
- `document:venture-augur/logos/competition-review/augur-logo-summary-email.md`: Augur is a local-first second brain that helps people and AI agents turn scattered context into useful judgment, memory, and action. Logo submission ties the name and icon to local-first second-brain positioning.
- `document:venture-augur/logos/competition-review/augur-short-description-pr.md`: files, notes, workflows, skills, and memory into a portable AI operating layer they own PR description defines Augur as an owned operating layer for second brains.
- `document:venture-augur/presentations/augur-edge-ai-deck.pptx`: Move your entire cognitive AI stack from the cloud to your Intel-powered device. Edge AI deck frames the product as local migration of the full cognitive stack.
- `document:venture-augur/presentations/augur-elevator-pitch.pptx`: A local-first system where your files — documents, spreadsheets, notes, configs — become executable. Elevator pitch links local-first data to executable workflows.
- `document:venture-augur/presentations/augur-elevator-pitch.pptx`: Companies rushing AI wrappers to market hit a wall every time models change. I’m building the layer underneath that survives regardless. Pitch differentiates Augur from model-specific wrappers.
- `document:venture-augur/presentations/augur-vision-1.pptx`: Your data lives on YOUR disk. Plain text. No cloud required. No proprietary formats. Vision deck states the sovereignty and readable-format principle.
- `vault:notes/venture/knowledge/startups/mvp-with-cursor.md`: Keep frontend + backend in one workspace. Cursor can trace everything. Splitting = losing context.
