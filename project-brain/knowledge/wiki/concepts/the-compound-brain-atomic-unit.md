# The Compound Brain — The Atomic Unit

## Compiled truth

The brain is the connecting layer: IT control above, nodes below. It directs each
node's agent and compounds their work back, bottom-up. It is on-prem, owned,
git-versioned, and composed per node — instructions, decisions, knowledge, skills,
and workflows. Agents (e.g. Claude Code) and models (cloud or local) are swappable;
the brain is the durable asset that persists and appreciates.

The key principle is "one brain, many nodes": a single brain can serve an AI PC, a
robot, and a server simultaneously — a team or a robot fleet on one owned brain.
Four layers compose the brain: instructions, decisions, knowledge, and skills/workflows,
all version-controlled and compounding. The brain's BRAIN.yaml manifest declares
type, id, root, and attached project — a minimal but complete identity that makes
every brain portable and discoverable.
